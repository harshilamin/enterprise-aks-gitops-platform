# Roadmap

## v1.0.0 — Foundation
Architecture, structure, governance, documentation, and CI.

## v1.1.0 — Secure sample API
FastAPI, tests, health endpoints, non-root image, graceful shutdown, scanning, and SBOM.

## v1.2.0 — Helm
Reusable chart, probes, resources, HPA, PDB, Service, ingress, NetworkPolicy, and tests.

## v1.3.0 — Argo CD
AppProject, ApplicationSet, environment desired state, promotion, drift detection, and rollback.

## v1.4.0 — Azure identity and secrets
Workload identity, Key Vault CSI, SecretProviderClass, and least-privilege access.

## v1.5.0 — Observability
OpenTelemetry, OTLP Collector, Prometheus, Grafana, logs, traces, alerts, and SLOs.

## v1.6.0 — Reliability
HPA, KEDA, disruption controls, topology spread, anti-affinity, and failure testing.

## v1.7.0 — Progressive delivery
Argo Rollouts, canary, blue/green, health analysis, and rollback.

## v1.8.0 — Supply-chain security
Image signing, provenance, policy enforcement, and admission controls.

## v2.0.0 — Final integrated platform
Complete platform, runbooks, interview walkthrough, and release automation.
